Frequently asked questions

What do I get each week?

Every Thursday at 6pm (Melbourne time), a new weekly drop lands in the app: 3 recipes, a prep plan and a shopping list. It's built so you can prep a few things on the weekend and eat well all week without thinking about it. Every past drop stays in your archive too, so the library keeps growing the longer you're a member.

Is this a meal plan or a diet?

Neither, really. There's no calorie counting and no cutting out food groups. Every meal follows the same simple plate I eat from myself: plenty of veg and fibre, good protein, smart carbs, a bit of healthy fat, and something fermented when it fits. It's how I think most people should be eating, made easy enough to actually stick with.

Is it suitable for vegetarians, people with allergies, or families?

Most recipes are easy to adapt, and I'll flag swaps where I can. If you have a serious allergy or a medical condition that affects what you can eat, check ingredients carefully and talk to your own doctor or dietitian. Recipes are written for 4 serves and scale up easily for families.

How long does prep take?

Most weeks, about 60–90 minutes of prep sets you up for the week. Individual meals are designed to come together in 30 minutes or less on a weeknight.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, straight from your account. No emails to send and no hoops to jump through.

What's the founders price?

For the first 7 days after launch, founding members can join for $15/month, and that price never goes up for as long as you stay a member. After that, it's $19/month or $149/year.

Is this medical advice?

No. I'm a doctor, but Doctor's Hour is general nutrition and recipe content, not personalised medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or take medication that's affected by diet, please check with your own doctor before making big changes.

Is there an iPhone app?

Right now Doctor's Hour runs in your browser on phone or desktop. You can save it to your home screen and it works just like an app. A proper App Store version is coming later, and your membership will carry over.
